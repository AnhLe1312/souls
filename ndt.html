Narrative Design Thinking

A methodology for helping emerging-tech companies find Narrative-Market-Fit when entering a new market.

Emerging technologies can travel globally, but the meaning people attach to them does not always travel with the product. Narrative Design Thinking (NDT) helps teams understand how a new market perceives, interprets, and responds to what they are building - and shape the narrative needed for that market to understand, trust, and adopt it.

Inspired by Design Thinking , NDT was developed specifically for emerging-tech companies navigating markets where existing categories, language, and mental models may not yet be enough.

Who it's for

Built for AI, Web3, Quantum, and other emerging-tech companies entering new markets where their existing product-market fit, reputation, or category does not automatically translate.

Applied NDT

Developed with academic rigor. Tested in real markets.

Narrative Design Thinking has been academically advised by professors from Waseda University and Stanford Graduate School of Business , and successfully applied with US-based emerging-tech enterprises entering Vietnam through Souls Lab .

Beyond market applications, NDT has been used with leading business schools, incubators, and accelerators across Vietnam, the US, and Singapore to help founders and builders navigate emerging technologies, new markets, and evolving market narratives.
